How to interpret results

  • Likely AI (≥65%): Text shows patterns typical of AI generation
  • Mixed / Uncertain (40-64%): Inconclusive — may be human-edited AI or formal human writing
  • Likely Human (<40%): Text shows human-like variation and unpredictability

How It Works

Multi-Provider Detection

Automatically uses the best available provider: lmscan (local, offline) → GPTZero API → Sapling API → Built-in heuristic

Statistical Analysis

Built-in heuristic analyzes sentence length uniformity, vocabulary diversity, AI-typical phrases, and transition word frequency

Limitations Acknowledged

Results include explicit limitations disclosure. AI detection is probabilistic — false positives and negatives occur, especially with formal, edited, or non-native writing
