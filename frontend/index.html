Similarity algorithms

Scores use measures such as Jaccard similarity on n-gram sets and longest common substring ratio. Higher overlap means more shared phrasing — for review, not automatic proof of misconduct.

Concepts introduced: text normalization, tokenization, n-gram / shingle pattern matching, set-based similarity (Jaccard), and substring matching. Results are indicators for human review.
